The roundtable addition · the Jacobian is objective. the metric is sovereign.

What the roundtable clarified

Every system uses G = J⊤WJ to describe the metric. But no one had named what W actually is. W is not a free parameter. W is a diagonal matrix whose entries are the weights a specific sovereign agent places on each dimension of the output space. The same Jacobian J, evaluated under different W, gives a completely different geodesic.

Why this is not a detail

Two patients with identical biomarkers can make opposite treatment decisions without either being wrong. They have different W. The ROYGBIV heatmap is not a property of the system. It is a property of the system seen by a particular sovereign. Red for one client may be violet for another. The heatmap is a portrait of values, not just sensitivities.

Musical reading

The same chord progression in the same key sounds tense to a classically trained ear and joyful to a gospel congregation. Same J. Different W. The pianist who reads the room is computing W in real time — not just the harmonic sensitivity, but which sensitivities matter to this audience, this moment.

The distinction from all other AI systems

Most systems optimize under their own W — the developer's, the platform's, the advertiser's. Ukubona's claim is that the digital twin hands the client their W and lets them walk the geodesic themselves. The twin computes the path. The sovereign chooses the direction. Sovereignty is the ability to set your own W.

The Jacobian is objective. The metric is sovereign.
G(θ) = J(θ)⊤ · W(θ) · J(θ)  —  where W encodes not what the system is, but what it means to the one who must decide.

Gated epistemic velocity · the roundtable correction

Original formulation (GPT)
v_E = −dH / dC

Bits of uncertainty removed per unit epistemic cost (tokens, compute, human attention). Mathematically cleaner than the earlier uncertainty × time / tokens. But still missing one thing.

The correction (Claude)
v_E = −dHgated / dC

Where H_gated is the entropy of the specific decision variable the client needs to act on — not total system entropy. A system that massively reduces irrelevant uncertainty while leaving H_gated untouched has high apparent epistemic velocity and zero practical value. Most consulting reports look exactly like this.

max η = v_N / v_E
= (−dL/dR) / (−dHgated/dC)

The digital twin's true objective: maximise the conversion of epistemic work into physical progress. Not merely reduce entropy — reduce the entropy that gates the Newtonian decision. v_N = real-world progress per unit resource (momentum per dollar). v_E = uncertainty removed per epistemic expenditure. The ratio η is what a good digital twin is actually maximising. The gospel pianist maximises this every Sunday: minimal rehearsal cost, maximal congregational resolution.

The premature formalisation warning: The rootless D9 functions as V9 in G major because the bassist is already holding the root — the systemic environment implies it. This formalism should wait until a specific client engagement demands a specific number, then be derived from that constraint rather than imposed from above. The ontology is ready. The operationalisation follows the scalar, not the other way round.
